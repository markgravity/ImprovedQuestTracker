# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First public release.
- Sort tracked quests by zone (current zone first), level, level reversed, title or distance.
- Move completed quests to the top or bottom.
- Sort menu on the tracker's Quests header, in the quest right-click menu, and on R3 when the
  tracker has controller focus.
- Sound when an objective of a tracked quest completes.
